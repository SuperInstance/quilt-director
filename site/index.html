What is this?

A substrate walker is a piece of software that walks a substrate (a corpus, a problem, a fleet) and emits witness receipts as it goes. The witness chain links them all together. quilt-trace reads those receipts and renders them as a self-contained HTML page with a force-directed graph of the chain.

The pattern is now ship-able: quilt-brewer grows new walkers from recipes, quilt-bootstrap restores a fresh sandbox in <90s, quilt-fleet-snapshot bakes state into a tarball. The doctrine is the artifact.
